Your claim was rejected because your details in EPFO records do not match your Aadhaar. This could be your name, date of birth, gender or father's name. Even a small difference, like a short form of your name, can cause this.

EPFO checks your claim against Aadhaar. When the two do not match, the claim stops. The fix is a Joint Declaration. This is a request to correct your details, signed by you and, in most cases, your employer.

How you file it depends on your category. Category A members can usually correct details themselves online. Category B members file online and the employer approves. Category C members file a paper form with two documents at the EPFO office.

Next steps:
1. Compare your name, date of birth and gender in your UAN profile with your Aadhaar card.
2. File the Joint Declaration for your category, using the checklist on this page.
3. After the correction is approved, file your claim again.
