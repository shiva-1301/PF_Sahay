Your claim was rejected because another claim of yours is already being processed. EPFO does not process two claims of the same type at the same time.

This often happens when a claim is filed again before the first one is finished.

The usual fix is to wait until the earlier claim is settled or rejected. If the earlier claim has been stuck for a long time, you can ask EPFO to finish or close it through a grievance.

Next steps:
1. Open Track Claim Status on the member portal and find the earlier claim.
2. If it has not moved for a long time, file a grievance on epfigms.gov.in quoting both claim IDs.
3. File a new claim only after the earlier one is closed.
