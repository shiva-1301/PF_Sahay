Your claim was rejected because of a tax document problem. Your PAN may not be verified, or a form to avoid tax deduction is missing.

If your total service is under five years and the amount is large, tax may be cut from your PF payment. From 1 April 2026, Form 121 replaces the old Forms 15G and 15H for people whose income is below the taxable limit. If PAN is not verified, more tax is cut.

The usual fix is to verify your PAN and, if it applies to you, submit Form 121 with your claim.

Next steps:
1. Check that PAN shows as verified under Manage, then KYC, on the member portal.
2. If your income is below the taxable limit, fill in Form 121.
3. File your claim again and upload Form 121 with it.
