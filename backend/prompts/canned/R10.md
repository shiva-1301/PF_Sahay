Your claim was rejected because of a problem with the pension part of your PF, called EPS. EPS is the Employees' Pension Scheme.

The cause depends on your case. Your employer may not have paid the pension share correctly. Your service may be too short for a pension withdrawal, which needs at least six months. With ten years or more, you cannot withdraw the pension money; you get a scheme certificate or a monthly pension later.

The usual fix is for your employer to correct the pension contribution, or for you to use the right pension form for your years of service.

Next steps:
1. Check your passbook for the pension (EPS) amounts and your total years of service.
2. If amounts are missing or wrong, send your employer the letter on this page.
3. File the correct pension form, and use a grievance on epfigms.gov.in if the problem continues.
