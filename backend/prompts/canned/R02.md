Your claim was rejected because EPFO does not have your Date of Exit. This is the date you left your last job. Without it, EPFO thinks you still work there.

Your employer is supposed to mark this date. Many employers forget or delay it. You cannot get your full PF money while EPFO thinks you are still employed.

There are two usual fixes. If your UAN is linked with Aadhaar, you can mark the Date of Exit yourself on the member portal, two months after the last month your employer paid PF. Otherwise, ask your employer to mark it. If they do not, you can complain on EPFiGMS and later file an RTI application.

Next steps:
1. Check the member portal for your last job's Date of Exit, and mark it yourself if the option is open.
2. If you cannot, send your employer the letter on this page asking them to mark it within 15 days.
3. If nothing changes, file the grievance on epfigms.gov.in, then file your claim again once the date shows.
