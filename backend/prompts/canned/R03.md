Your claim was rejected because the dates of your jobs overlap. EPFO sees that you joined your new job before you left the old one, according to their records.

This usually happens when an old employer enters a wrong Date of Exit, or a new employer enters a wrong Date of Joining. EPFO cannot count the same months twice.

The usual fix is to correct the wrong date. The employer who made the mistake corrects the exit date, or you file a Joint Declaration to correct the joining date. After that, the transfer or claim can be filed again.

Next steps:
1. Check the Date of Joining and Date of Exit for each job in your service history on the member portal.
2. Send the letter on this page to the employer whose date is wrong.
3. After the date is corrected, file your transfer or claim again.
