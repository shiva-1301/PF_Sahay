Your claim was rejected because your PF service history is not complete. You may have more than one UAN, or old PF accounts that were never moved to your current UAN.

Every time you change jobs, your old PF should be transferred to your current account. If it is not, EPFO cannot see your full service, and some claims stop.

The usual fix is to transfer the old accounts using Form 13 on the member portal. If an account was linked to your UAN by mistake, your employer can ask EPFO to remove it.

Next steps:
1. Check Service History on the member portal and note any old accounts or UANs.
2. File a transfer request with Form 13 for each old account.
3. When the transfers show in your passbook, file your claim again.
