Your claim was rejected because of a problem with your bank account details. The account may not be verified, the IFSC code may be wrong, or the name in the bank may not match your PF records.

EPFO only pays into a bank account that is linked to your UAN and verified. The account must be in your own name. A joint account is allowed only with your spouse.

The usual fix is to update the bank details under KYC on the member portal, check the IFSC code, and get the account approved by your employer or bank.

Next steps:
1. Check your bank account and IFSC under Manage, then KYC, on the member portal.
2. Correct the details, upload a cheque or passbook if asked, and ask your employer to approve them.
3. When the bank shows as verified, file your claim again.
