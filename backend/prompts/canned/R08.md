Your claim was rejected because you are not yet eligible for it, or you used the wrong form.

For a full PF withdrawal (Form 19), you must be out of work for at least two months. Pension forms depend on your years of service. Form 10C is for less than 10 years. With 10 years or more, you get a scheme certificate or a monthly pension later through Form 10D. Advances (Form 31) are allowed only for certain purposes.

The usual fix is to wait until you are eligible, or file again on the correct form.
