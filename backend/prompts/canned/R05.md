Your claim was rejected because your Aadhaar is not properly linked to your UAN. UAN is your Universal Account Number for PF. Either Aadhaar is not linked at all, it is not verified, or the wrong Aadhaar number is linked.

Most online PF services need a verified Aadhaar link. EPFO uses it to confirm who you are.

If Aadhaar is simply not linked, you can usually link it on the member portal. If the wrong Aadhaar is linked, your employer has to request the correction through a Joint Declaration, which the EPFO office then approves. If your employer's company has closed, you submit a paper Joint Declaration at the EPFO office.

Next steps:
1. Check under Manage, then KYC, on the member portal whether Aadhaar shows as verified.
2. Link Aadhaar yourself, or send your employer the letter on this page asking them to correct it.
3. Once Aadhaar shows as verified, file your claim again.
